About Us

Sweet Cake Academy is a professional cake training and baking supplies business dedicated to nurturing creativity and excellence in baking. We provide practical training to beginners, aspiring entrepreneurs and experienced bakers who wish to improve their skills.


Our Mission

To empower individuals with practical cake baking and decorating skills while providing high-quality baking tools and equipment that support creativity and success.


Our Vision

To become the leading cake training academy and baking supplies provider by producing skilled bakers and inspiring innovation in the baking industry.


What We Offer

  • Professional cake baking training.
  • Advanced cake decoration classes.
  • Cake business and entrepreneurship training.
  • Quality baking equipment and decorating supplies.
  • Hands-on practical learning with experienced trainers.
